HOME, WORK, ANIMALS
Plan for loss of heat, power, communications, and resupply lasting more than a day. NWS recommends keeping a flashlight and batteries, a battery-powered radio, shelf-stable food and water, needed medicines, first-aid supplies, and essentials for children and pets. Prepare a safe heat source and use it with proper ventilation. Test smoke and carbon-monoxide alarms; do not operate a generator in an enclosed space. Keep animal feed, water, and sheltered areas in mind.

VEHICLE
Before a winter trip, check vehicle fluids, lights, heater, and wipers. Tell someone your route and expected arrival; check current road conditions before departure. Carry warm clothing and blankets, food and water, a flashlight, first-aid supplies, a shovel, scraper, traction material, and basic tools. Do not rely on this offline map or a phone as your only navigation or communication method.

FIELD LIMIT
This list is not exhaustive. Match preparations to local climate, household needs, vehicle, and official emergency-management advice. Follow current evacuation or travel restrictions.
