SAFETY AND SCOPE
This is an edited, offline summary of federal guidance. It is not an official NWS publication, forecast, alert, evacuation-zone map, or substitute for local emergency instructions. It cannot receive changing storm information. When possible, use current NWS and local emergency-management information and NOAA Weather Radio. If local officials order an evacuation, leave promptly and follow their route and destination instructions.

PREPARE AND ACT
• If you live in a storm-surge risk area, be ready to evacuate and allow sufficient time to pack and inform family or friends before leaving.
• Learn local hazards and plans before a storm threatens. Secure windows before the pre-storm rush if you have appropriate shutters or fitted exterior-grade plywood.
• Keep a battery-powered NOAA Weather Radio and a way to receive local instructions. This offline computer does not provide live warnings.
• Follow local officials. A general guide cannot determine whether your home is safe or whether you must evacuate.

IF YOU ARE NOT ORDERED TO EVACUATE
• Shelter in a small interior room, closet, or hallway on the lowest level. Put as many walls as possible between you and the outside.
• Stay away from windows, skylights, and glass doors.
• Do not assume the danger is over if winds briefly calm as the eye passes. Winds can rapidly return from the opposite direction.
