Why some catalog items are missing

Project N.O.M.A.D. can offer additional archives, detailed map regions, software updates, and other optional downloads. Those items are not part of this test payload; downloading them requires a separate internet-connected update session. The installed chat model, archive, and map data remain usable offline.

This test edition contains no reviewed first-aid or emergency guidance. Do not use generated answers as a substitute for authoritative medical or safety references.
