Maps

Why some catalog items are missing

Project N.O.M.A.D. can offer additional archives, detailed map regions, software updates, and other optional downloads. Those catalog downloads require a separate internet-connected update session. Other FieldOS content packs can be transferred by USB and installed without internet.

The included CDC references and FieldOS-edited NWS-based summaries are source-attributed and added to N.O.M.A.D.'s local Knowledge Base. They are not comprehensive emergency guidance and do not provide live weather alerts. NOAA/NWS does not endorse FieldOS. A keyword-based FieldOS guard blocks many safety-related prompts, but it cannot recognize every phrasing; do not use AI answers for emergency, medical, water-treatment, food-safety, or evacuation decisions. Open the source library and consult appropriate official guidance.

Add an offline content pack

Verified map and reference packs can be copied to a USB drive or local folder with their matching .sha256 file. Open FieldOS Install Content Pack from Applications, choose the .tar.zst archive, and approve the administrator prompt. Keep the archive and its checksum sidecar together.

  1. Open Files and browse to the USB drive or folder that contains the pack archive.
  2. Open FieldOS Install Content Pack from Applications → Utilities, choose the archive, and authenticate when prompted. FieldOS verifies the checksum and manifest before installation.
  3. For a map pack, open or reload Maps; for a reference pack, use the Source Library and N.O.M.A.D. Knowledge Base. The importer reports when indexing finishes.

A valid checksum confirms file integrity, not trustworthiness or redistribution rights. Review the pack's source, date, and license. Open original source documents when safety decisions depend on them; AI summaries can be incomplete or incorrect.
